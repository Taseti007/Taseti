About Taseti Media

A platform for truth, consciousness, and African liberation through knowledge and peaceful resistance

A Fundamental Reminder

This world was constructed by white supremacists on a foundation of racial hierarchy...

Historically, African men and those of African descent have been targeted through violence...

Before leading others or giving life, we must first learn to govern ourselves. Self-control, focus, skill...

Self-respect is the baseline of all engagement with the world...

Africa existed long before modern borders, colonial divisions, and imposed national identities...

Racism is systemic—embedded in institutions, laws, education, media...

Reading, learning, and intellectual development are forms of resistance...

Our ancestors endured unimaginable suffering so that you could exist today. Honoring them requires deliberate action...

They have little or no moral values. They do not respect us...

Who I Am

I am Warren Yabi, and I publish analysis and synthesis content...

My journey took me from France, where I spent twenty years...

Faced with this dead end, I almost fell...

"I have chosen a third path: peaceful resistance through knowledge."

Having lived in both countries, I can say without ambiguity...

Throughout my life, I have been surrounded by people of all backgrounds...

I am not alone in this situation...

Our Mission

I am against all forms of discrimination and violence...

What We Denounce

Neocolonialism in all its forms—economic, monetary, military, cultural...

African dictators, heads of state, and political and military elites...

Corporations, financial institutions, and foreign powers...

All those who participated in crimes against humanity...

What We Stand For

I am not here to call Africans to turn against other peoples...

I will not let other communities disrespect our ancestors...

We stand for:

  • Truth over comfortable narratives
  • Accountability over historical amnesia
  • African sovereignty and self-determination
  • Economic independence and cooperation
  • Cultural preservation and intellectual development
  • Strategic resistance through knowledge and consciousness

On Collective Responsibility and Moving Forward

I don't believe Europeans today should be held personally responsible...

To move forward, we need to acknowledge the truth of our shared history...

Our personal identities are not our ancestors' actions...

Yet, while we acknowledge this, we must also recognize that systems of power...

Western powers, through control of media, education, and international institutions...

The injustice is not just historical—it is ongoing, structural, and carefully maintained.

Ultimately, the challenge is to face reality honestly...

Only by acknowledging history, forgiving where necessary...
