Taseti Media: Independent African Analysis

Taseti is an independent media outlet born from a simple observation: Africa does not lack intelligence, resources, or history. What it lacks are clear, demanding, and strategic spaces to think about its future beyond imposed narratives.

This space is not religious but spiritual, political, and conscious. Africa was one of the founding pillars of human thought long before history was confiscated, rewritten, and whitewashed.

The objective is to put an end to domination, predation, and hypocrisy. Colonialism has not disappeared—it has changed form. It persists through debt, extraction, technological dependence, media narratives, and social fragmentation.

2026: The End of Automatic Obedience

For centuries, Africa has been expected to follow rules it did not write, adopt models it did not choose, and obey directives framed as unavoidable. In 2026, this automatic submission begins to collapse.

A generation that is informed, connected, and disillusioned has identified the truth: dependency is not destiny. It is a system. And systems can be dismantled once they are exposed.

Africa is not at war with the world. Africa is at war with submission.
