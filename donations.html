Why Your Support Matters

Taseti is not funded by governments, corporations, churches, NGOs, or political parties. We receive no institutional backing, no advertising revenue, and no grants that come with editorial conditions. This independence is not accidental—it is deliberate, and it is essential to our mission.

Your support ensures that our analysis remains credible, our investigations remain fearless, and our conclusions remain honest. We do not moderate our findings to please funders. We do not soften our language to protect advertisers. We do not avoid uncomfortable truths to maintain access to power.

What Your Donations Support:

  • Research and Investigation: In-depth historical research, fact-checking, and documentation require time and resources
  • Writing and Analysis: Producing comprehensive, well-researched articles that challenge dominant narratives
  • Technical Infrastructure: Hosting, security, and maintenance of our platform
  • Long-Term Archiving: Preserving historical documentation for future generations
  • Independence: Maintaining editorial freedom from all external pressures

Our Commitment to You

Complete Editorial Independence: Donations do not influence our coverage, conclusions, or editorial choices. We write what needs to be written, not what funders want to hear.

Transparency: We are accountable to our readers, not to institutional funders with hidden agendas.

No Paywalls: All Taseti content remains freely accessible. Knowledge should not be gatekept by wealth.

Historical Integrity: We document truth, preserve memory, and refuse to sanitize history for contemporary comfort.

Why Independence Matters

Most media organizations depend on advertising, corporate sponsorship, or institutional grants. These funding sources create invisible editorial pressures:

  • Advertisers influence coverage through the threat of withdrawal
  • Corporate sponsors expect favorable treatment for their industries
  • Government grants come with expectations of political alignment
  • Foundation funding often carries ideological strings

Taseti rejects all of these compromises. Supporting Taseti is not charity—it is a decision to protect independent thought, preserve historical truth, and ensure that at least one platform exists where analysis is not filtered through the interests of power.

What We Will Not Do

We will not:

  • Moderate our findings to protect powerful interests
  • Accept funding that compromises editorial independence
  • Soften historical truths for contemporary sensibilities
  • Hide behind false balance when evidence is clear
  • Prioritize clicks and engagement over accuracy and depth
  • Gatekeep knowledge behind paywalls

Your support makes this possible. With reader funding, we answer only to truth and our audience—not to advertisers, governments, or institutional donors.
