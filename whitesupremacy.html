White Supremacy: The Global System Targeting Black People

Understanding how white supremacy was constructed, how it operates, and how it systematically oppresses, exploits, and erases Black and African people worldwide

This page documents the systematic targeting of Black and African people by white supremacy. White supremacy is not individual racism—it is a global system constructed deliberately to position Europeans as the "prototype race" while subjugating, exploiting, and attempting to erase Black people from history, humanity, and the future. Understanding this system is essential to dismantling it.

Pigmentation and the Colonial Double Standard

Today, it is widely accepted without real resistance that ancient Africa was neither homogeneous nor static. Emphasis is placed on the diversity of its peoples, climates, and historical trajectories. But this recognition abruptly stops the moment it threatens another, far more protected narrative: that of an originally white Europe. This silence is neither accidental nor scientific. It is political.

If Africa was never of a single color, then Europe was not either. This conclusion is elementary, yet it remains treated as a sensitive, almost taboo subject. To acknowledge that ancient Europe was not uniformly white is to shatter one of the symbolic pillars of colonial domination: the idea of a natural, original, self-evident European whiteness.

Genetic data show that traits associated with very light skin became widespread in Europe only recently. They do not represent an original state, nor a universal condition. Modern European whiteness is a recent biological construction. To deny this is not scientific rigor—it is a narrative safeguard designed to preserve a colonial imagination.

It is also important to remember that Europe was called Eurasia once—it is one continent. They "separated" the continent to expand into North Africa and East Africa that they call the "Middle East." This is how they have been able to call us Africans "sub-Saharans" and all sorts of names for the last centuries. This is also how they have been able to create their system built on racism. They have geopolitically manufactured races by breeding us, they forced themselves on anyone that did not look like them.

The Fear of Genetic Dominance: Miscegenation and Control

While love and sex are often intertwined, they are not the same. Love is a multifaceted emotion capable of profound fulfillment, while sex is primarily a physical expression that can be both pleasurable and complex. People may engage in sexual activity without experiencing love, yet reproduction can still occur. If this happens on a large scale, concerns about overpopulation and the inheritance of genetic traits arise.

For instance, the darker skin gene is genetically dominant over lighter skin, meaning that successive generations of mixed populations could result in darker skin becoming more prevalent. Some people recognized these dynamics and began to fear the mixing of genes—a fear that can be traced back to the Moorish era.

The Moors and the Origins of Racial Fear

The Moors, Black Muslims from North Africa, established themselves in parts of Spain and Portugal. While they did not "invade" in a destructive sense, they did mix with the local populations. White women were reported to find Black men attractive just as they did white men, which provoked fear among white men that they might become a minority in their own lands.

This fear of racial mixing is historically known as miscegenation, defined as the mixing of different racial groups through marriage, cohabitation, sexual relations, or procreation. Miscegenation was common during the Moorish era. Moors often had white wives or concubines, leading to the birth of mixed-race children.

Religious Propaganda Against Miscegenation

The elites, the church, and certain groups began spreading propaganda against miscegenation, framing it as a sin and against divine will. Religious texts, such as the Bible, were misinterpreted to suggest that humans should remain racially "pure." For example, they claimed Adam and Eve were both white, and therefore all humans should be white, conveniently ignoring the fact that the Bible does not specify the skin color of their children, Cain and Abel.

As the Moors were not the only wealthy Black Africans, fear extended to all Black people. Propaganda portrayed Black Africans as savages, inferior, and even cursed by God. The biblical story of Ham, son of Noah, was manipulated to claim that Black Africans were divinely destined to serve others, citing that Ham saw his father naked. Yet, the scriptures also note that Shem and Japheth saw Noah naked as well, but they were not cursed—a fact deliberately ignored.

This widespread propaganda directly facilitated the transatlantic slave trade. Millions of Black Africans were captured, enslaved, and transported to the Americas, forced to work on plantations and in mines, treated as property, and denied basic human rights. The church and government collaborated to maintain this system, consolidating power by keeping the white population dominant and the Black population subjugated, while simultaneously promoting fear of miscegenation.

Arabs Were Never the Moors

It is also important to clarify historical misconceptions: the Arabs were never the Moors. The Arabs took advantage of the Moors' presence in the Iberian Peninsula, collaborating at times to expand Islam and gain wealth, while systematically undermining African civilizations and enslaving Black Africans. Their objectives were strategic and economic, rather than religious.

Religion as a Tool of Power and Control

Caucasians used religion to justify their actions, but their true goals were power and wealth. They sought to dominate the world, impose the beliefs they made up, and exploit our resources. Colonies were established to expand their empires, and conversion to Christianity was often a tool to gain more followers and control. They never respected our ways, beliefs, traditions, or cultures, seeing them as inferior, and they consistently attempted to impose their values on others.

Religion was a weapon used to manipulate minds. They dehumanized those who were different, creating myths and stereotypes to justify oppression. Fear, guilt, and shame were employed to control populations, while an "us versus them" mentality fueled division and conflict. History was rewritten to erase the creations of others, and education and media were used to indoctrinate people and promote their ideology.

Through religion, they created a sense of identity, loyalty, and purpose among their followers. People were not savages—they had to be convinced. It justified their actions as righteous, provided a moral framework, and instilled a sense of divine right, superiority, and entitlement. Religion was used to create obedience, fear, and devotion, while offering hope, salvation, and community.

Ultimately, religion was not about faith or spirituality but about power. It allowed Europeans to dehumanize others, exploit populations, maintain control, and establish colonies while justifying all their actions as morally and divinely sanctioned.

The Foundation: Europeans as the "Prototype Race"

White supremacy emerged as a deliberate European ideological project to position Europeans as the "prototype race" of humanity. This narrative constructed Europeans as the origin, standard, and reference point against which all others were measured. Through control of history, religion, science, and education, Europeans inserted themselves into ancient narratives and redefined global history to justify conquest, colonization, and exploitation.

Brutality, genocide, enslavement, and cultural erasure were not anomalies—they were the logical consequences of an ideology that framed domination as natural, progress as European, and hierarchy as inherent.

The construction required several coordinated strategies:

  • Claiming ancient civilizations: Appropriating Egyptian, Mesopotamian, and other African/Asian achievements as "Western" or "Caucasian"
  • Creating false histories: Inventing Greek and Roman origins for European civilization while denying African contributions
  • Religious justification: Using Christianity to declare Black people cursed by God and destined for servitude
  • Scientific racism: Creating pseudoscientific racial classifications that placed whites at the top and Blacks at the bottom
  • Historical erasure: Systematically removing African achievements from records and attributing them to Europeans or external influences

The Global Anti-Black System: Discrimination Everywhere

If you are African in Europe, America, Australia, or Asia, you will be discriminated against. This is not occasional or incidental—it is systematic, constant, and built into every aspect of society. You will not be able to get the same jobs as everyone else. You will always be judged differently because of your skin color. They will make fun of you, bully you, exclude you, exploit you, and harm you. All of this is legal because their laws are structured to hurt Black people.

Employment Discrimination

Black people are systematically passed over for jobs, promotions, and opportunities regardless of qualifications. Identical resumes with white-sounding names receive more callbacks than those with Black-sounding names—this has been proven repeatedly through studies, yet nothing changes. Black people are paid less than whites for the same work. Black professionals are assumed to be less competent than white colleagues. Black businesses receive less funding and fewer opportunities than white-owned businesses.

Educational Targeting

Black children are disciplined more harshly than white children for the same behavior. Black students are tracked into lower-level classes regardless of ability. Black academic achievement is attributed to affirmative action rather than merit. Black scholars and intellectuals are marginalized, their work dismissed or stolen by white academics who claim credit for Black ideas.

Housing Discrimination

Black people are denied mortgages, charged higher interest rates, steered away from desirable neighborhoods, and subjected to discriminatory rental practices. Neighborhoods where Black people live are systematically devalued, under-resourced, and over-policed. Property owned by Black people is worth less than identical property owned by whites simply because of the race of the owner.

Healthcare Brutality

Black people receive inferior medical care, are not believed when they report pain or symptoms, die at higher rates from treatable conditions, and are used as experimental subjects without informed consent. Black maternal mortality rates are catastrophically high compared to white rates, even for wealthy, educated Black women. The medical establishment treats Black bodies as expendable.

Criminal "Justice" System

Black people are arrested more often, charged more severely, convicted more readily, and sentenced more harshly than whites for identical offenses. Black people are killed by police at rates far exceeding their population percentage. Black communities are over-policed while white communities engaging in the same behaviors are left alone. The entire system—from policing to prosecution to imprisonment—is designed to control, brutalize, and eliminate Black people.

Constant Mockery and Dehumanization

They will make fun of you. They will bully you. This happens everywhere Black people exist in white-dominated societies. Black physical features are mocked—skin color, hair texture, facial features, body types. Black names are ridiculed. Black accents are imitated mockingly. Black culture is either stolen and appropriated or dismissed as inferior. Black intelligence is questioned. Black humanity is denied.

The mockery starts in childhood. Black children are bullied by white children, teachers, and administrators for their appearance, for their culture, for existing while Black. They are told their hair is unprofessional, ugly, or against school dress codes. They are punished for wearing traditional clothing or hairstyles. They are made to feel ashamed of their African heritage.

Black cultural contributions are stolen and commodified by whites while Black creators receive no credit or compensation. White people wear Black hairstyles, adopt Black music and dance, imitate Black speech patterns, and profit from Black culture while simultaneously mocking and discriminating against actual Black people.

Living on Stolen Land Built by Murdered Ancestors

We are using a system that has been built because of the killing of our ancestors. Every aspect of life in Europe, America, Australia, and other white-dominated societies is built on stolen African land, stolen African labor, stolen African resources, and stolen African lives. The wealth, the infrastructure, the institutions, the opportunities—all of it was created through the genocide and enslavement of Black people.

In America, every building, road, farm, and city was built using enslaved African labor or built on land stolen from indigenous peoples. The entire economy was built on plantation slavery that killed tens to hundreds of millions of Africans. The industrial revolution was funded by profits from slavery. American wealth is African wealth that was stolen through genocide.

In Europe, the Renaissance, the Enlightenment, the Industrial Revolution, the colonial empires—all were built on stolen African knowledge and wealth extracted from African slavery and colonization. European cities were built with profits from the slave trade. European museums are filled with stolen African artifacts. European prosperity is entirely built on African blood.
